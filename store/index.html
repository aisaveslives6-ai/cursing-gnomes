Apple makes you do these 5 taps yourself, in your Settings — no app or website can do them for you. It's about 5 minutes, once. Tick each off as you go:

  1. Open SideInstaller and install the DNS profile.
    Open SideInstaller →
  2. Pick a certificate on that page and install SideInstaller. (If one certificate is dead, try the next.)
  3. Trust it: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the profile, tap Trust.
  4. Open SideInstaller, connect the VPN as it shows you, and sign in with your Apple ID inside the app — never type your password into a website.
  5. Tap Install SideStore inside SideInstaller. You can delete the DNS profile afterwards.

Set-and-forget refresh

Never tap refresh manually again.
SideStore can renew your apps on a schedule, all by itself:

  1. In SideStore → Settings, turn on Background Refresh.
  2. For bulletproof: open the Shortcuts app → Automation tab → + → Time of Day (pick a dead hour, like 4 AM).
  3. Choose the Refresh All Apps action from SideStore, set it to Run Immediately with no notification. Done — your signatures renew themselves every night.

Good to know

What does the store automate?
One-tap installs, the source feed with update notices, and the 7-day expiry countdown with a refresh button. The signing itself happens inside SideStore with your Apple ID — Apple gives no other way on a stock iPhone.

Why does it expire every 7 days?
Apple’s rule for free Apple IDs, not ours. Your installer re-signs apps on its own — saves are never touched.

Is this safe?
Every app is signed on your phone with your Apple ID. Nothing here can see your password, and the game is fully offline.

Want it to never expire?
Apple’s $99/year Developer Program signs apps for a full year. Same store, same games.
