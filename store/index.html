Good to know

What does the store automate?
One-tap installs, the source feed with update notices, and the 7-day expiry countdown with a refresh button. The signing itself happens inside SideStore/AltStore with your Apple ID — Apple gives no other way on a stock iPhone.

Why does it expire every 7 days?
Apple’s rule for free Apple IDs, not ours. Your installer re-signs apps on its own — saves are never touched.

Is this safe?
Every app is signed on your phone with your Apple ID. Nothing here can see your password, and the game is fully offline.

Want it to never expire?
Apple’s $99/year Developer Program signs apps for a full year. Same store, same games.
