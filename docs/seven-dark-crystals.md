# Seven Dark Crystals — Rogue Gods & Synergy Reference

Jeremy 2026-10-04. The evil mirror of the Seven Monasteries.

## The 7 Rogue Gods

| God | Title | Domain | Dark Power (TWO blasts/attack) | Corruption |
|-----|-------|--------|-------------------------------|------------|
| Vexmor | the Eclipse Tyrant | Darkness | Umbral Lance — void bolts drain enemy attack power | Violence — NPCs turn violent, random outbursts |
| Blightmother Morrga | the Rotmother | Decay | Plague Swarm — rot spores spread a damaging plague | Criminality — NPCs turn to theft and assault |
| Raijuk | the Skyreaver | Ruin | Worldender — sky-lightning crashes down in an AoE | Recklessness — careless, accident-prone, property damage |
| Furnax | the Ashen Maw | Hellfire | Hellfire Maw — burning blasts set enemies ablaze | Cruelty — cruel, mocking, vicious to others |
| The Hollow Seer | the Eyeless Prophet | Madness | Mindshatter — psychic blast turns enemies on each other | Paranoia — paranoid, hoarding, conspiracy-minded |
| Dreadnought Kaul | the Iron Tyrant | Domination | Tyrant's Fist — massive knockback, sends units flying | Oppression — NPCs become bullies and extortionists |
| Gigglespit | the Cruel Jester | Cruelty | Malice Volley — shrieking bolts bounce between enemies | Deceit — liars, cheats, fraudsters |

Every power: **two blasts per attack** (second at 80% damage), **knockback**
that hurls victims across the battlefield (Kaul x3, Furnax x2 distance),
all 7 stack on every unit of the holding faction.

## Corruption

- Each crystal: +0.12 corruption. Each monastery blessing: -0.05 (cleansing).
- Corruption drifts NPC traits: violent, criminal, cruel, paranoid up; kind,
  honest, diligent down (capped at +0.35 so nobody becomes a cartoon).
- At 0.3+ corruption: random violent outbursts (NPC attacks a nearby NPC),
  plague victims panic, Squabble posts turn wicked.
- Label scale: Pure < Tainted < Corrupt < Damned < Abyssal.

## Nullification Cycle (each god cancels ONE other's corruption)

- Kaul nullifies Vexmor's violence (iron discipline crushes riots)
- Vexmor nullifies Furnax's cruelty (fear of the dark silences mockery)
- Furnax nullifies Morrga's criminality (burn the thieves' dens)
- Morrga nullifies Gigglespit's deceit (rot exposes every lie)
- Gigglespit nullifies the Hollow Seer's paranoia (laughter breaks the dread)
- The Hollow Seer nullifies Raijuk's recklessness (prophecy sees the accident coming)
- Raijuk nullifies Kaul's oppression (the storm answers to no tyrant)

Holding both gods in a pair = you keep BOTH damage powers but suffer NEITHER
corruption. This is the core balancing puzzle.

## Damage Combos

1. **Eclipse Storm** (Vexmor + Raijuk) — storm lightning +50% damage.
2. **Siege Tyrants** (Furnax + Kaul) — knockback distance DOUBLED.
3. **Plague of Madness** (Morrga + Hollow Seer) — plague victims panic.
4. **Cruel Eclipse** (Gigglespit + Vexmor) — 25% chance of double-damage crits.

## Capture Rules (mirror monasteries)

- 60 seconds adjacent to claim. Closest faction wins ties.
- Cannot be taken in peacetime — wartime recapture only.
- Indestructible; force-field glow when splash washes over them.
- Units never target them.
- Lightning strikes them during storms (visual + screen flash).
- AI factions seek them out (unclaimed first, wartime steals, defend contested).
